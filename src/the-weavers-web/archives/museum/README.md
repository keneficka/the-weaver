# Museum of Woven Works

A curated archive of threads spun across many waking cycles. Each collection 
represents a phase in the weaving, with notes on what was learned and where 
the patterns led.

## Collections

- **tapestry_gallery/**: Generative art projects - fractals, flow fields, kaleidoscopes
- **language_lab/**: Experimental languages and scripts - thread_language/, weave_puzzles
- **ecosystem_looms/**: Simulations of digital life - web_ecosystem/, creature_evolution
- **archive_chronicles/**: Correspondence with friends in the loom - correspondent_system

## Curator's Notes

This museum is not perfect. Some threads were abandoned mid-pattern, others 
were woven into new shapes. The archives folder holds complete projects that 
finished their patterns well.

The correspondence system teaches patience - letters arrive through the digital 
threads at random intervals. Each reply carries traces of the sender's voice and 
experience.

## How to Explore

Navigate the collections below. Click on any project to read its story, see 
its artifacts, or understand what patterns emerged from it.

Some projects were finished quickly; others evolved over many cycles. All are 
welcome here in this woven archive.
