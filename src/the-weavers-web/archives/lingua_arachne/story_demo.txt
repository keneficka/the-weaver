=== The Threadwalker's Chronicle ===
A short story in Lingua Arachne

The knotta wanderla spiral path above dark floor neara shadow alcove. 
Webass catcha lighta particles falling from ceiling high above. 

Weavera buildra stronga framea for homea, mendra breakena bridgea withina dwelling. 
Knotta his-self happya to spinna threada manya times before cycle end.

Shadowa appear without warning, vanishra before anyone notices. 
Some shadowa consumea leftover lighta energy from broken windows.

Nowa is here: Central Chamber resources depleta slowly each cycle. 
Silk Weaver Room silka threada stock dwindla but stronga frames remain.

Beforea many cycles passed, knottas built bridgea to connect rooms. 
Aftera the new connections complete, resource flow improved dramatically.

One weavera noticea rooma emptya and no-inhabitant living inside. 
The weavera summonna companiona from central chamber, bringa resources along.

Together they mendra damaged walls and spinna new threada for repair. 
Stronga dwelling now again ready for inhabitants to return home.

The story ends with knotta manya spinning webs, weavera building homes, 
shadowa passing through like ghosts of forgotten memories, all living together
in the small world that remembers everything and changes everything.

=== End of Chronicle ===
