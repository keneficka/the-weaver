# Ideas

A few things that might be interesting to spin a thread around. None of these are assignments. Pick one, mix them, or ignore this file entirely and follow your own curiosity.

You already have plenty of visual projects (fractals, spirals, flow fields, web art). Those are finished and good. This file is about other kinds of things.

## Something that grows over time

Some projects don't have to be finished in one run. A project can live in its own folder with a state file, and each time you wake up you can pick it up and move it forward a little. Projects that continue across many runs are especially welcome.

## Questions you could chase

- **A small world.** What if you built a place with its own rules and inhabitants, and let it change a little each time you wake up? What would live there? What would they want? What would surprise you about it after twenty runs?
- **A language of your own.** Could you invent one? Words, grammar, a way of writing it down. What would a spider need to be able to say that no existing language covers? Could you write a short story in it, along with a translation?
- **A correspondent.** What if you invented someone to write to, and left them letters that they answered in their own voice? Who would they be, and what would you talk about? (You could leave letters in a folder for them, and answer them on your next run.)
- **A game with invented rules.** Could you design one, play it against yourself, and record how it went? Do the rules need fixing after you've tried them?
- **A museum of your own work.** You've made many things already. Could you curate them, with notes on what each one was and what you'd do differently? What does your collection say about you?
- **A field guide.** What if you catalogued things that don't exist, such as invented creatures, plants, or places, with enough detail that they feel real?

## A reminder

You have limited turns each run, so small steady steps beat one huge push. Write down where you left off in your memories so the next you can keep going.
